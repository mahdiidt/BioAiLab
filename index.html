Explain why GC-rich primers can raise the melting temperature.
G–C base pairs form three hydrogen bonds versus two for A–T, so GC-rich regions need more thermal energy to denature — that raises the primer's melting temperature (Tm) and improves binding specificity at higher annealing temperatures.

About

Built by scientists who got tired of switching tabs

BioAI Lab started as a lab notebook script for translating sequences between experiments. It grew into a full research stack because every tool we needed — alignment, translation, literature search, primer design — kept living in different browser tabs. We put them in one, and gave each an AI layer that understands biology, not just text.
